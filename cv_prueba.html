What I do at Centum

Product management

I act as a product owner / product manager for the product line.

  • Analyse feature requests coming from customers and from internal teams.
  • Discuss the feasibility of each feature with the departments involved.
  • Prioritise the features and group them into change requests.
  • Developer tasks hang from each change request, adapted to what the analysis showed was feasible.
Feature request→ Analysis & feasibility→ Priority & grouping→ Change request→ Developer tasks

Requirements and mockups

  • Write the requirements for each new functionality created through a change request.
  • Keep traceability from high-level needs down to detailed specifications, using IBM DOORS Next Generation.
  • Build HTML mockups so developers understand what product is asking for.

Technical documentation

  • Create and maintain documentation for customers and for internal use: manuals, declarations, analyses and more.
  • Automated the documentation workflow in LaTeX. Everything is defined with macros and custom commands, so documents are consistent and quick to update.

Safety

Our products are aeronautical, so safety documents and analyses are a big part of the job.

  • Fault Tree Analysis (FTA).
  • MTBF and RAMS (Reliability, Availability, Maintainability, Safety) analyses.
  • EMI analysis and equipment risk analysis in aircraft environments.
  • Work done following aeronautical standards, to support airworthiness and certification.
FTAMTBFRAMSAirworthiness
